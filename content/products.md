## ForecraftAI

**ForecraftAI** is an AI-powered forecasting solution designed to help **Quick Service Restaurant (QSR)** chains improve revenue planning, reduce inventory waste, and optimize staffing.

Our system builds **custom models** for each store or region using your historical data, enabling accurate, location-specific forecasts that evolve with changing trends.

---

### Key Features

- **Store-level AI models** trained on your data  
- **Weekly forecasts** delivered via dashboard or API  
- **Monthly model retraining** to adapt to new trends  
- **CSV and API support** for data upload and integration  
- **Multi-store dashboard** for regional and chain-wide visibility  

---

### Business Impact

ForecraftAI helps QSR brands:

- Predict **sales trends** with greater accuracy  
- Reduce **overstocking and food wastage**  
- Align **staffing levels** with demand  
- Improve **supply chain and procurement planning**  

---

### Built for Scale

Whether you manage 10 stores or 1000, ForecraftAI delivers consistent, reliable insights without the need for internal ML expertise. Our cloud-based architecture ensures fast deployment and easy access across teams.
