## About NiveshAI Technologies

**NiveshAI Technologies Pvt Ltd**, based in Hyderabad, provides AI and machine learning forecasting solutions designed specifically for the **Quick Service Restaurant (QSR)** and **food service** industries.

We help businesses streamline operations through **AI-driven predictive models** and **IoT-enabled solutions**, enabling better decision-making and improved efficiency.

## Our Focus

We address core operational challenges faced by QSR chains:

- Forecasting **sales trends** for accurate revenue planning  
- Predicting **inventory requirements** to reduce wastage  
- Aligning **staffing needs** with demand  
- Optimizing **procurement and stock levels**  

Our models are customized for each location, retrained regularly, and delivered through a simple dashboard or API.

## Key Advantages

- Industry-specific AI forecasting tools  
- High accuracy and adaptability across regions  
- Scalable cloud infrastructure  
- No in-house ML expertise required  

## Our Product

Our flagship product, **[ForecraftAI](/products)**, is a forecasting solution built for QSR chains. It helps brands improve planning, reduce waste, and increase operational efficiency across multiple locations.
